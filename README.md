# AnalyticNumberTheory-Lean

A structured, `sorry`-free Lean 4 + Mathlib (`v4.36.0`) library and interactive [Verso](https://github.com/leanprover/verso) online encyclopedia for Analytic Number Theory, grounded in 潘承洞、潘承彪《解析数论基础》 and organized purely by mathematical theme.

- **Online Encyclopedia**: https://lixiang90.github.io/AnalyticNumberTheory-Lean/
- **Repository**: https://github.com/lixiang90/AnalyticNumberTheory-Lean

## Structure

- `AnalyticNumberTheory/` — Formal Lean 4 modules organized into ten thematic domains:
  1. `AnalyticTools/` — Farey fractions and period dissections, integral transforms, summation formulas (Abel, Euler–Maclaurin, Poisson), Gamma & Stirling, complex function bounds (Jensen, Borel–Carathéodory, Hadamard three-circles, Phragmén–Lindelöf), finite-order entire functions, Hadamard factorization, and sharp inverse-power sums at individually spaced points.
  2. `DirichletSeries/` — Convergence abscissae, uniqueness & Landau's theorem, arithmetic convolutions, Euler products, Perron's formula, exponential smoothing by Mellin inversion, Ramachandra’s two-contour formula with a finite-order polar correction and rapid decay in height, vertical order & mean value theorems.
  3. `RiemannZeta/` — Functional equation, sharp two-sided approximate functional equations for Riemann and real-shift Hurwitz zeta including a log-free error on the closed strip for ordered cutoffs and the real Hardy cosine sum with balanced critical-line error of order |t|^(−1/4), pole, convexity and Weyl subconvexity bounds, Hadamard product & zero expansion, zero counting, de la Vallée Poussin, Littlewood and Vinogradov–Korobov zero-free regions, Riemann Hypothesis equivalences, the two-term critical-line second moment with remainder of order √T log T, the corresponding fixed-shift Hurwitz critical-line expansion with linear coefficient γ−ψ(a)−1, the Hurwitz zeta mean square $T\zeta(2\sigma,a)$ for fixed real shifts $0<a\le1$ and $1/2<\sigma\le1$, Ingham’s fourth-moment asymptotic with leading coefficient $1/(2\pi^2)$, and Hardy’s theorem on infinitely many zeros on the critical line. The balanced Hurwitz approximate functional equation on the critical line includes the first Riemann–Siegel correction with an $O(t^{-3/4})$ remainder uniform in the shift, with corresponding Hardy and periodic L-function formulas.
  4. `PrimeDistribution/` — Chebyshev functions, Mertens' theorems, the explicit formula, the Prime Number Theorem with Vinogradov–Korobov error $O(x\exp(-d(\log x)^{3/5}(\log\log x)^{-1/5}))$ for some $d>0$, oscillation, the uniform Siegel–Walfisz theorem for primes in progressions, Ingham’s zero-density exponent $3(1-\sigma)/(2-\sigma)$ and Huxley’s exponent $12(1-\sigma)/5$, log-free zero-density estimates for zeta and for families of primitive Dirichlet characters, the prime number theorem in intervals of length $x^\theta$ for every fixed $7/12<\theta<1$ and a quantitative Chebyshev estimate near that threshold, the maximal Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem for ψ, θ and π, and Linnik’s polynomial least-prime theorem.
  5. `DirichletLFunctions/` — Dirichlet characters & primitive conductors, Gauss sums, Pólya–Vinogradov character sums, functional equations, sharp direct and dual polynomials for arbitrary periodic coefficients and a conjugate dual polynomial for primitive characters, with a log-free error on the closed strip for ordered cutoffs, a conductor-uniform Riemann–von Mangoldt formula, zero expansions, zero-free regions, Page and Siegel theorems, conductor-uniform Deuring–Heilbronn repulsion and Turán high-derivative zero detection, critical-line and right-strip mean squares for arbitrary fixed periodic coefficients, the two-term critical-line mean square for individual primitive characters with its explicit prime-divisor correction, height-aspect Weyl bounds and conductor-uniform Vinogradov growth estimates. A numerical lower bound at one for real nonprincipal characters gives an explicit separation for every real zero, including a possible exceptional zero.
  6. `ExponentialSums/` — Weyl differencing, sharp truncated Poisson summation, reciprocal-phase stationary integrals with endpoint errors, van der Corput A/B processes, arbitrary-order derivative tests & exponent pairs, Vaughan's identity & linear prime exponential sums, Vinogradov’s integral and solution-count identity, the leading diagonal constant $s!$ for $s\le k$, inhomogeneous and affine power-sum systems, nonsingular prime-power congruence counts, the classical Vinogradov mean value theorem with explicit constants in the degree, bilinear polynomial sums, Vinogradov bounds for polynomial phases with a rational approximation to the leading coefficient, and Hua's lemma.
  7. `AdditiveNumberTheory/` — Circle-method identities, Goldbach singular series, the weighted ternary asymptotic with arbitrary logarithmic saving, the asymptotic for ordered triples of odd primes with error $O(N^2/\log^4 N)$, and Vinogradov’s three-primes theorem. An independent proof isolates the possible Page exceptional conductor and gives a finite weighted estimate and an unweighted error of order $O(N^2/(\log N)^{17/5})$. For two primes, the number of positive even exceptions up to N is O_A(N/(log N)^A) for every natural A. Waring topics include integral and local representation counts, discrete Fourier inversion, Chinese remainder factorization and an Euler product of local densities for the singular series for degree `k` and `s > 2k` variables. The library also proves local solubility for prime powers coprime to the degree when `s > k`, a Fourier inversion proof that the real factor at one equals `Γ(1+1/k)^s/Γ(s/k)` in that range, and the full circle-method asymptotic with error `o(n^(s/k−1))` for `s > 2^k` and `k ≥ 2`. A uniform positive lower bound for the singular series when `s ≥ 4k` yields the Hilbert–Waring theorem for every positive degree. Diminishing ranges and Vinogradov’s minor-arc estimate give the quantitative asymptotic basis bound $G(k)\le 16k\log k+4k+2$.
  8. `DivisorProblems/` — Dirichlet's hyperbola identity over semirings, average orders of arithmetic functions, Jacobi's two-square theorem, the $O(x^{1/3})$ Gauss circle discrepancy for squared radius $x$, the truncated Voronoï cosine expansion with uniform error for real cutoffs, and van der Corput’s divisor error estimate $O(x^{27/82}(\log x)^2)$.
  9. `SieveMethods/` — Legendre's prime-counting formula, Buchstab's identity, Brun and Selberg sieves, exact Rosser remainders with the fundamental lemma at large sieve parameter, the Dickman and Buchstab functions, the Rosser upper and lower linear sieve with its explicit divisor-error budget and a uniform $O((\log\log y)^{-3/10})$ error in the sieve functions, Kuhn’s lower bound for primes whose nonzero even shift has at most three prime factors counted with multiplicity, the normalization and factorial convergence bounds of the sieve functions, analytic and arithmetic large sieves with local spacings and denominator-sensitive Farey weights, Brun–Titchmarsh estimates with coefficient two for every interval of length greater than the modulus, the implication from any fixed improvement of the Brun–Titchmarsh coefficient on a power range to a real zero-free interval of width $c/\log q$, a two-step contraction for the Rosser lower sieve, a prime-supported hybrid large sieve, hybrid mean values of Dirichlet polynomials and the Halász–Montgomery large-values inequality.
  10. `ModularAndPartitions/` — complex-weight Eisenstein lattice sums, their analytic continuation by Lipschitz summation and their modular transformations with the complex-weight cone sum with its Barnes double integral and all-weight Hankel contour and the weight-two correction, Dedekind sums & reciprocity, Dedekind $\eta$-function modular transformations, partition generating functions, modular inversion, the Hardy–Ramanujan saddle-point asymptotic and the exact Rademacher expansion with a truncation bound uniform in the partition index and cutoff. The library identifies the first relative correction to the Hardy–Ramanujan asymptotic and proves that the unit circle is a natural boundary for the partition generating function. The canonical logarithm of η transforms exactly under every integral modular substitution.
- `Encyclopedia/` — Human-readable Verso online encyclopedia (`VersoManual`) with KaTeX math, live typechecked docstrings, cross-references, citations, and searchable index.

## Reading the formalization

The encyclopedia introduces the ten themes in Chinese. For example, its Waring article derives
the exact integral for the number of ordered representations by nonnegative powers, and its
divisor article evaluates the hyperbola identity at a finite cutoff. Each displayed Lean
declaration links the mathematical statement to the hypotheses used by the library.

The library proves the Hardy–Ramanujan leading term by a saddle-point argument and the
Bombieri–Vinogradov mean estimate by Vaughan's identity and the large sieve. Coverage
is still in progress. The library also proves the exact Rademacher series and an unconditional
zeta zero-density bound. It proves Linnik's polynomial bound for the least prime in each reduced residue class.
Stronger zero-free regions and sharper prime estimates in short intervals remain in progress.

## Building

```bash
lake exe cache get
lake build AnalyticNumberTheory Encyclopedia
lake run verso/versoMain EncyclopediaMain.lean
```

The generated multi-page HTML encyclopedia is written to `_out/html-multi/`.
